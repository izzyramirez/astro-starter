## Let's stack headings

### We should make sure that looks good, too.

Sometimes you have headings directly underneath each other. In those cases you often have to undo the top margin on the second heading because it usually looks better for the headings to be closer together than a paragraph followed by a heading should be.

#### We haven't used an h4 yet

But now we have. Please don't use **h5** or **h6** in your content, Medium only supports two heading levels for a reason.

We are not styling them out of the box because **h4** elements are already so small that they are the same size as the body copy. What are we supposed to do with an **h5**, make it _smaller_ than the body copy? No thanks.
